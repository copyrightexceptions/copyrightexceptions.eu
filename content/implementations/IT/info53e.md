description: "This exception allows for the reproduction of works or excerpts from works for public safety purposes, in parliamentary, judicial, or administrative procedures, provided that the source and, where possible, the name of the author are indicated."

- any user

- public safety purposes

- reproduction

- works
- performances
- phonograms
- film fixations
- broadcasts
- audiovisual fixations in sporting events
- press publications
compensation:
- no compensation required
attribution:
- the source and, where possible, the name of the author must be indicated
otherConditions:
remarks: "The Italian implementation of art. 5(3)(e) of the InfoSoc Directive is restricted in terms of types of usage, covering reproduction only.<br /><br />The Italian law differs between exceptions, where use is exempt from the rightsholder's authorisation and no payment is due, and limitations, where payment of equitable compensation is due.<br /><br />Under art. 71decies, exceptions and limitations to authors’ right apply also to the neighbouring rights. In addition, para 16 of art. 43-bis (press publishers’ right) explicitly references the exceptions and limitations in Chapter V of Title I."
